# Text-to-Video Diffusion Models - Complete Paper List

Comprehensive chronological list of influential T2V diffusion papers.

## 2022 - Foundation Year

### Video Diffusion Models (VDM) ⭐
- **Authors**: Ho, Salimans, Gritsenko, Chan, Norouzi, Fleet
- **Affiliation**: Google Research
- **ArXiv**: [2204.03458](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.03458) (April 2022)
- **Key Innovation**: First video diffusion, 3D U-Net, reconstruction guidance
- **Status**: ✅ [Full Analysis Available](./01-Video-Diffusion-Models)

### CogVideo
- **Authors**: Hong, Ding, Zheng, Liu, Tang
- **Affiliation**: Tsinghua University
- **ArXiv**: [2205.15868](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.15868) (May 2022)
- **Key Innovation**: Large-scale transformer T2V, multi-frame-rate hierarchical training
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

### Make-A-Video ⭐
- **Authors**: Singer, Polyak, Hayes, Yin, An, Zhang, et al.
- **Affiliation**: Meta AI
- **ArXiv**: [2209.14792](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14792) (September 2022)
- **Venue**: ICLR 2023
- **Key Innovation**: No text-video pairs needed, pseudo-3D convolutions
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

### Imagen Video ⭐
- **Authors**: Ho, Chan, Saharia, Whang, et al.
- **Affiliation**: Google Research
- **ArXiv**: [2210.02303](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02303) (October 2022)
- **Key Innovation**: 7-model cascade, v-parameterization, 1280×768 output
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

### LVDM (Latent Video Diffusion)
- **Authors**: He, Yang, Zhang, Shan, Chen
- **Affiliation**: HKUST, Tencent AI Lab
- **ArXiv**: [2211.13221](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.13221) (November 2022)
- **Key Innovation**: 3D latent space, 1000+ frame videos
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

### MagicVideo
- **Authors**: Zhou, Wang, Yan, et al.
- **Affiliation**: ByteDance
- **ArXiv**: [2211.11018](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.11018) (November 2022)
- **Key Innovation**: Efficient latent generation, adaptor layers
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

---

## 2023 - Rapid Advancement

### Align Your Latents (Video LDM) ⭐
- **Authors**: Blattmann, Rombach, Ling, Dockhorn, et al.
- **Affiliation**: Stability AI, NVIDIA
- **Venue**: CVPR 2023
- **ArXiv**: [2304.08818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08818)
- **Key Innovation**: Temporal layers on frozen T2I, autoregressive long video
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

### Text2Video-Zero
- **Authors**: Khachatryan, Movsisyan, Tadevosyan, et al.
- **Affiliation**: Picsart AI Research
- **Venue**: ICCV 2023 (Oral)
- **ArXiv**: [2303.13439](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13439)
- **Key Innovation**: Zero-shot video generation, no training required
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

### AnimateDiff ⭐
- **Authors**: Guo, Yang, Rao, Wang, et al.
- **Affiliation**: CUHK, Shanghai AI Lab
- **ArXiv**: [2307.04725](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.04725) (July 2023)
- **Key Innovation**: Plug-and-play motion module, MotionLoRA
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

### ModelScope Text-to-Video
- **Authors**: Wang, Yuan, Chen, Zhang, et al.
- **Affiliation**: Alibaba Group
- **ArXiv**: [2308.06571](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.06571) (August 2023)
- **Key Innovation**: Open-source weights, foundation for ZeroScope
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

### LaVie
- **Authors**: Wang, Chen, Ma, et al.
- **Affiliation**: Shanghai AI Lab, NTU, CUHK
- **ArXiv**: [2309.15103](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.15103) (September 2023)
- **Venue**: IJCV 2024
- **Key Innovation**: Cascaded LDM, Vimeo25M dataset, RoPE
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

### VideoCrafter1
- **Authors**: Chen, Xia, He, et al.
- **Affiliation**: Tencent AI Lab
- **ArXiv**: [2310.19512](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19512) (October 2023)
- **Key Innovation**: Open diffusion models, 1024×576 resolution
- **Status**: 📝 Planned

### Stable Video Diffusion (SVD) ⭐
- **Authors**: Blattmann, Dockhorn, Kulal, et al.
- **Affiliation**: Stability AI
- **ArXiv**: [2311.15127](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.15127) (November 2023)
- **Key Innovation**: Three-stage training, data curation, open weights
- **Status**: 📝 Planned
